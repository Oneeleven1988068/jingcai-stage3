Patch: **Hybrid CRS Satellite Bet Construction**

The patch separates two questions that were previously entangled:

- **Direction layer:** Is the match suitable as an A1/A2 direction leg?
- **CRS opportunity layer:** Does the exact-score distribution contain a separately justified center/tail opportunity that may serve as a small high-payout satellite?

The key governance addition is true cost accounting: if the satellite has multiple score selections or an A2 anchor has multiple outcomes, the nominal pass label can dramatically understate the number of primitive bets.
