# In-Match Shock Attribution Policy

- Final result always remains in ordinary OOS outcome scoring; shocks never erase a loss.
- A red card / severe in-play structural shock may be recorded separately for causal attribution.
- `PREMATCH_DIRECTION_CLEAN_FALSIFICATION=NO` may be used only when a verified in-play event materially changes the state space.
- Counterfactual result without the shock is `UNOBSERVABLE`; never claim the pre-match direction was therefore correct.
- In-play shocks cannot be used to retrofit pre-match weights or remove inconvenient samples.
